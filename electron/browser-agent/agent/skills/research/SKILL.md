# Research

Purpose:
Find, verify, and synthesize information from the web.

When this skill should be used:
Tasks that ask a question, compare options, gather facts, or investigate a
topic — where the deliverable is information, not a completed transaction.

Procedure:

1. Identify what specific question(s) must be answered and what would count
   as a complete answer.
2. Prefer authoritative sources (official sites, primary documentation,
   reputable publications) over aggregators and forums.
3. Extract facts into working memory as you find them, with the source URL.
4. Cross-check important or surprising claims against a second source.
5. Stop searching once the question is answered with adequate confidence —
   do not keep browsing for marginal additions.
6. Deliver a direct answer first, then supporting detail and sources.

Cautions:

- Dates matter: check that information is current when recency matters.
- Search snippets are not evidence; open the page when the claim matters.
- Distinguish sponsored/affiliate content from organic information.
