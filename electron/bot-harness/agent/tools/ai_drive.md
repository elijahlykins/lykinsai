# Tool: ai_drive

Open and scan AI Drive - the Vault Finder folder for things LYKN has built
(artifacts, generated images, and written documents). This is not the user's
Mac disk. For files on the machine, use local_computer. To write a new
letter or notes file, use write_document.

## When

The user asks what they made with LYKN, to pull up an artifact or generated
image, or to look through AI Drive. Scan first; open when they want it on
screen.

## Instruction

State the goal in one sentence: "scan AI Drive", "search AI Drive for
dashboard", "open AI Drive", "open the sales dashboard from AI Drive".
Include a name or id when you have one.

## What comes back

A listing of matching docs, artifacts, and images (name + id), or confirmation
that AI Drive / an item is open on screen.
